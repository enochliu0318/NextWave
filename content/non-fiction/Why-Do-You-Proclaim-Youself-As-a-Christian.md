There are several reasons that support my claim to be a Christian.

First, also the most important, I believe that I am a sinner and only Jesus can save me from hell. In Romans 3:28, Paul explained: “For we hold that one is justified by faith apart from works of the law.” Since I have faith in Him and believe that only he can save me, I’m justified, and God has accepted me as His child. Second, God works in me through the Holy Spirit. Paul also said that the Holy Spirit will pray for us and work through us; whoever has faith in him shall have the Spiritual fruits. (Romans 8:26, Galatians 5:22-23) My feeling of love for God’s words and the desire to be close with Him is from the holy spirit. And through my behavior – with no pride – I’m different from the world that shows me that I don’t belong to the world. Finally, I proclaim myself as a Christian as God promised in the Bible, I proclaim in my speech that Jesus is Lord and have faith in our hearts that Jesus’ resurrection from the dead, we shall be saved. (Roman 10:9) While I admit that Jesus is the King of my life, and even if I need to face the force from the world, I proclaim myself as a Christian, God will recognize me from heaven. Even though I am a Christian which will never change, I still need to strive for sanctification, and the stength for me to fight with my human nature which I trust I’ll win at the end of the world is from God.
